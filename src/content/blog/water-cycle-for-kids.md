# Water Cycle for Kids: A Fun and Easy Guide

Have you ever wondered where rain comes from? Or why puddles disappear on a sunny day? It's all because of something amazing called the **water cycle**! Let's learn about it together.

## What is the Water Cycle?

The water cycle is like a big adventure that water takes over and over again. Water travels from the oceans to the sky, then back down to Earth, and then back to the oceans. It's like a never-ending journey!

## The 4 Steps of the Water Cycle

### 1. Evaporation - Water Goes Up!

When the sun shines on water in oceans, lakes, and rivers, something magical happens. The water gets warm and turns into tiny invisible drops called **water vapor**. These tiny drops float up into the sky!

**Think of it like this:** When you leave a wet towel in the sun, it dries up. Where did the water go? It evaporated into the air!

### 2. Condensation - Clouds Form!

When water vapor goes high up in the sky, it gets very cold. The cold air turns the water vapor back into tiny water droplets. These droplets stick together and form **clouds**!

**Think of it like this:** When you breathe on a cold window, you see fog appear. That's condensation!

### 3. Precipitation - Water Falls Down!

When clouds get too heavy with water droplets, the water has to come down. This is called **precipitation**. It can be:
- **Rain** - water drops
- **Snow** - frozen water crystals
- **Hail** - balls of ice
- **Sleet** - tiny ice pellets

### 4. Collection - Water Gathers Together!

When rain or snow falls, it collects in different places:
- Oceans and seas
- Lakes and ponds
- Rivers and streams
- Underground (groundwater)

And then... the cycle starts all over again!

## Fun Facts About the Water Cycle

- The water you drink today might have been drunk by a dinosaur millions of years ago!
- A single drop of water can spend 3,000 years in the ocean before evaporating.
- About 97% of Earth's water is in the oceans.
- Plants help the water cycle too! They release water vapor through their leaves.

## Try This at Home!

**Make Your Own Water Cycle:**
1. Put some water in a clear plastic bag
2. Tape the bag to a sunny window
3. Watch what happens over a few days
4. You'll see the water evaporate, condense on the bag, and "rain" back down!

## Quick Quiz

**Question:** What happens when water vapor cools down in the sky?
**Answer:** It condenses and forms clouds!

**Question:** What is it called when water falls from clouds?
**Answer:** Precipitation!
